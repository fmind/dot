# Assessment Record

Apply the relevant checklist items and output contract below.

## Review checklist

- **Rights and restrictions**: commercial use, seats/affiliates, API/automation, scraping, benchmarking/publication, security testing, reverse engineering, prohibited sectors/uses, geography, and export controls.
- **Software, models, and content**: copying, modification, redistribution, network/SaaS use, attribution/notices, source disclosure, copyleft compatibility, patents, trademarks, and distinct rights over code, weights, datasets, inputs, and outputs (read actual license text, not repo metadata/scanners alone).
- **Data and AI use**: confidentiality, provider access, training/improvement rights and opt-out scope, retention/deletion (logs and backups included), residency/transfers, subprocessors, personal/regulated data bars, and required DPAs/BAAs; verify technical controls separately from contract promises.

## Output contract

Scale depth to complexity:

- **Verdict and scope**: `Supported` (evidence backs stated requirements, not blanket legal clearance), `Conditional` (known, achievable obligations), `Not supported` (explicit conflict), or `Unresolved` (missing material evidence; list alongside conflicts when both exist), dated for the exact product/plan/version and use.
- **Context and assumptions**: entities, jurisdictions, data, activities, requirements, exclusions, and unknowns.
- **Evidence matrix**: requirement | document & clause | finding & interpretation | status | action & verification (link public URLs inline; use non-sensitive labels for private contracts).
- **Next steps**: ranked blockers, conditions with owners, counsel/vendor questions, re-review triggers, and a dated source register.
